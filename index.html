FAQ .

What is GLITCH?

GLITCH is a collection of 555 minimal monochrome glitch artworks.
Traits: Clean signals. Heavy static. Critical errors. Signal Tears. light interfaces. Data Collapses. Anamoly.

Which chain is GLITCH on?

Robinhood Chain, an EVM-compatible network. Chain ID 4663. The network uses ETH for gas. Confirm network details from official documentation before mint launch.

Is the mint free?

Yes. The mint is planned to be free, with a limit of one NFT per wallet. Final terms will be confirmed by the deployed smart contract.

How do I join the whitelist?

Enter your X username, complete the required social tasks, submit your public wallet address, and confirm.

Do I need to connect my wallet?

No wallet connection or signature is needed for allowlist registration. Never provide a seed phrase or private key.

When does mint begin?

The mint date and official mint link will be announced through GLITCH's official channels once confirmed.
